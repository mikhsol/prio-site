Frequently asked questions

Is Prio cloud-based?

No. Prio is designed for local-first usage and does not require a cloud account for core planning workflows.

Do I need a subscription to use Prio?

No. The free tier is fully usable. Pro and Lifetime expand limits and advanced capabilities.

Is this just another to-do list?

Prio is focused on decision quality: prioritization, execution order, and daily follow-through, not just task storage.

Who is Prio best for?

Knowledge workers, creators, and founders who handle many competing tasks and care about privacy.

How can I support Prio?

Install the app, leave a review on Google Play, or send crypto to the donation addresses below. Every bit of support helps keep Prio independent and privacy-first.

Will Prio come to iOS or desktop?

It's on the roadmap. Android is the current focus to get the core experience right. Follow launch updates for news on other platforms.
