Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 21, 2026 · Last Updated: February 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Prio ("the App") is a privacy-first, AI-powered task management application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Our core principle: Your personal data stays on your device. Prio is designed to be offline-first, with all task content, goals, and AI processing handled entirely on your phone.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Never Sent to Us)

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device. We never have access to it:

  • Tasks: Task titles, descriptions, due dates, priorities, categories, and completion status
  • Goals: Goal titles, descriptions, milestones, and progress
  • AI Classifications: Eisenhower Matrix classifications generated by on-device AI
  • Briefings: Daily morning and evening briefings generated on-device
  • Calendar Data: Read-only access to your device calendar (only when you grant permission) — used to show events alongside tasks; never transmitted
  • User Preferences: App settings such as theme, default views, and notification preferences

All locally stored data is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher (AES-256 encryption).

2.2 Information Collected Automatically (Anonymous)

We collect limited anonymous data to improve app stability and understand feature usage:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Contact information
  • Photos, files, or documents
  • Browsing history

2.3 Information We Never Collect

  • No account required: You can use Prio without creating an account or providing any personal information.
  • No personal identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • No device identifiers: We do not collect hardware IDs, advertising IDs, or device fingerprints for tracking.
  • No location data: Prio does not request or use location permissions.
  • No contacts: Prio does not access your contact list.
  • No user-generated content: Your tasks, goals, notes, and AI briefings are never transmitted from your device.

4. On-Device AI Processing

Prio uses on-device artificial intelligence to classify tasks, generate briefings, and provide productivity insights.

  • All AI processing happens on your device. No task content is sent to external servers for AI processing.
  • AI models used: Google Gemini Nano (via Android AI Core, on supported devices) and llama.cpp (bundled with the app as a fallback). A rule-based classifier also operates entirely on-device.
  • No cloud AI in the free or Pro tiers. Your data never leaves your device for AI purposes.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

The only data transmitted from your device is:

  • Anonymous crash reports (to Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Anonymous usage analytics (to Firebase Analytics)

No user-created content (tasks, goals, briefings) is ever transmitted.

7. Data Storage and Security

  • Local storage: All user-created data is stored exclusively on your device.
  • Encryption: User data is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher (AES-256 encryption).
  • No cloud storage: Prio does not store your data on any external server (in the current version).
  • No account: No passwords, credentials, or authentication tokens are stored.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Your data, your control: Since all personal data is stored on your device, you have complete control over it at all times.
  • Delete data in-app: Use Settings → Delete All Data to permanently erase all tasks, goals, and preferences.
  • Delete via Android: Go to Android Settings → Apps → Prio → Clear Data to remove all app data.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling Prio removes all locally stored data from your device.
  • Analytics data: Anonymous analytics data retained by Firebase is not linked to you and cannot be individually deleted. Firebase retains aggregated analytics for up to 14 months.

10. Children's Privacy

Prio is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children. Our target audience is adults (18+).

11. In-App Purchases

Prio offers optional in-app purchases (Pro subscription and Lifetime purchase). All payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not collect, process, or store any financial information. Your purchase history is managed by your Google Play account.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Know what data we have about you — since all personal data is on your device, you already have full access.
  • Deletion: Delete all your data at any time (see Section 8).
  • Portability: Export your data (available to Pro users).
  • Opt-out of analytics: You can disable Firebase Analytics by restricting background data for the app in Android Settings, or by using a device with no internet connection.
  • Withdraw consent: Revoke any optional permission at any time via Android Settings.

For EU/EEA residents: You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For significant changes, we will provide notice within the app.
